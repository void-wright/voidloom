# Code of conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, critique ideas rather than people,
and keep discussion focused on the work. Harassment, personal attacks and
publishing someone else's private information are not acceptable in issues, pull
requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour in a project space concerns you, contact the maintainer
privately through GitHub's
[private reporting](https://github.com/void-wright/voidloom/security) or by
mentioning that you need a private channel in a minimal issue (no details).
Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, close
threads or block participants who break these rules.
